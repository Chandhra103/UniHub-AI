# Reference notes

## AICTE National Internship Portal

Source: https://internship.aicte-india.org/ (visited 2026-11-01)

The page title identifies the National Internship Portal by AICTE and the Ministry of Education, Government of India. Its desktop landing page has a compact government/portal header, Explore / Govt Internships / Campus navigation, Login and Register actions, a recent-posts strip, and a cream-toned hero with a prominent orange internship search action. The search collects role/company and city/state. The landing page then presents a large “Our internships are as Indian as paani-puri” heading and a multi-column directory of government and employer programme cards. Examples observed include Indian Army, TULIP, CPWD, PRATIBHA, Maharashtra, Bharatiya Bhasha Samiti, Cisco Networking Academy, VTU, AMRUTAM, NHIDCL, AICTE Research, Edunet, Microsoft, Salesforce, OPPO, Autodesk, Internshala, MongoDB, NHAI, Google, MeitY, NASSCOM and Code for GovTech. This is secondary context for the UniHub internship/opportunity area, not a replacement for its teal profile-dashboard shell.
